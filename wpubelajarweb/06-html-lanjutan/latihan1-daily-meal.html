Healthy Eating Habits

  • Breakfast: Eat a balanced meal to start the day.
  • Lunch: Combine carbohydrates, protein, and vegetables.
  • Dinner: Eat a satisfying meal and avoid skipping meals.
  • Snacks: Choose fruit, yogurt, or nuts if hungry between meals.
  • Hydration: Drink water regularly throughout the day.

Daily Nutrition Tips

Remember!

A healthy diet is about balance, consistency, and enjoying nutritious food. Adjust portions to your needs, eat when hungry, and avoid extreme restrictions.
